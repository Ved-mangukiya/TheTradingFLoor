Trading Floor Championship — Rules & Handbook

1. Virtual Capital & Portfolios

Every team begins with official virtual capital. You can place market orders to buy and sell blue-chip stocks during active trading sessions. Cash balances update atomically upon execution.

2. Authoritative Valuation & Scoring

Net Worth = Available Cash + (Holdings Quantity × Current Market Price).
Return % = ((Final Net Worth − Starting Capital) ÷ Starting Capital) × 100.
Final championship standings are determined by verified total Net Worth at the end of Day 3.

3. Circuit Breakers

If any stock rises or falls by 20% from its official Day Open reference price, trading in that stock is immediately halted to protect the market. Halts can only be released by the Host Matrix Controller.

4. Breaking News Shocks

Macro-economic and company-specific news releases trigger gradual price movements over 30–60 seconds, weighted by each company's sensitivity (Beta). Monitor the wire feed closely to anticipate market reactions.

5. Market Pauses & Disconnections

If the host pauses the market, prices freeze and orders are rejected. If your connection drops, simply refresh: your session token and portfolio are preserved safely on the exchange engine.
